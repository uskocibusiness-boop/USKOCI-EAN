**Description (1007/4000):**

USKOČI connects people who need practical help with people who can take on local tasks.

POST A TASK
Describe what needs to be done, add relevant details and review applications from people who can help. Compare proposals before choosing a participant.

FIND AN OPPORTUNITY
Explore tasks on a map or in a list. Find opportunities that suit your skills, equipment, availability and area. Submit an application with your proposed terms.

KEEP AGREEMENTS CLEAR
Once an application is selected, the Agreement brings together accepted terms, important changes and messages in one place.

USKOČI includes task listings, map and list search, applications, agreements, messaging, work profiles, user reporting and support.

Task performance and payment terms are agreed directly between participants. USKOČI does not itself perform the listed tasks or guarantee the outcome of an agreement.

Initially intended for use in Serbia. Support, privacy information and account deletion details are available at uskoci.rs.
